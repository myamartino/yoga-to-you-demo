Private yoga at home

One-on-one sessions in the comfort of your own space.

Private yoga sessions are the most personalized way to practice. We'll design sessions around your specific goals, your body, your schedule — and you never have to leave home.

Why private sessions?

Private yoga gives you undivided attention and a practice designed entirely for you. Whether you're brand new to yoga, recovering from an injury, managing chronic pain, or looking to deepen your practice, private sessions offer a level of customization that group classes simply can't.

You set the pace. You choose the focus — whether that's strength, flexibility, stress relief, breath work, meditation, or something else entirely. And you get real-time feedback and adjustments to help you build a safe, sustainable practice.

What to expect

Each private session begins with a brief check-in about how you're feeling, what you'd like to focus on, and any areas of tension or discomfort. From there, I guide you through a practice tailored to your needs that day.

I bring all necessary props (mat, blocks, bolster, blankets, straps) and adapt to whatever space you have — a living room, bedroom, garage, backyard, wherever works for you.

Sessions are typically 60 or 75 minutes, and I offer flexible scheduling including early mornings, evenings, and weekends.

Who this is for

  • Complete beginners who want to learn foundations
  • People recovering from injury or managing chronic conditions
  • Those with anxiety around group classes or public spaces
  • Busy professionals with unpredictable schedules
  • Anyone seeking deeper personalization

All experience levels and bodies welcome. I work with clients in their 20s through their 80s, from active athletes to those with limited mobility.

Flexible options

You can book private sessions as one-off experiences, or we can set up a regular weekly or biweekly schedule. Many clients find that consistency helps build a sustainable practice, but there's no pressure to commit long-term.

I serve the greater Portland metro area and surrounding communities. Travel fees may apply for locations outside the core service area — just ask when you reach out.
