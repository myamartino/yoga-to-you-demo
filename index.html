About

Founded by Lauren Fields. Yoga To You was created to remove the barriers of traditional studios and reimagine how yoga fits into everyday life. Instead of asking clients to come to us, we bring everything needed for a seamless experience, anywhere. From living rooms and office spaces to rooftops, retreats, and paddleboards.

Yoga isn't about perfection, it's about presence.
