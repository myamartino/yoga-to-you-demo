Meet Lauren

Yoga To You was founded on the belief that yoga should be accessible, personal, and free from intimidation. I'm Lauren — a Portland-based yoga teacher with over 12 years of experience bringing yoga to people in their homes, workplaces, and communities.

I hold a 500-hour yoga teacher certification, specialized training in trauma-informed practices, and certifications in restorative and yin yoga. But more than credentials, I bring genuine care and attention to every session.
