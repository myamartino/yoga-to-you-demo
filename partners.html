Bring this model to your city

Interested in starting a similar yoga service in your community?

The Yoga To You model — personalized, trauma-informed, go-to-them yoga — works. If you're a yoga teacher interested in building a similar business in your city, or if you're looking for consulting on how to make yoga more accessible in your community, I'd love to talk.

Why this model works

Traditional yoga studios can be intimidating, expensive, and inaccessible for many people. By bringing yoga to people's homes, workplaces, and communities, you remove those barriers.

This model allows you to build deep relationships with clients, charge sustainable rates, and create a business that's flexible and location-independent. You're not tied to a studio lease or a packed class schedule — you design your own career.

Over the past decade, I've refined this approach and built a thriving business. I've learned what works, what doesn't, and how to build something sustainable without burning out.

What I can offer

If you're a yoga teacher (or aspiring teacher) who wants to build a similar business in your community, I offer consulting and mentorship on:

  • How to structure your services and pricing
  • Marketing and client acquisition without a big budget
  • Building long-term client relationships
  • Workplace yoga logistics and contracts
  • Trauma-informed, accessible teaching practices
  • Managing the business side (scheduling, insurance, contracts)

This is not a franchise or certification program — just one-on-one consulting for teachers who want to build something similar in their own way.

Who this is for

This consulting is for certified yoga teachers (or those actively in teacher training) who want to start or grow a mobile yoga business. You should have a solid personal practice, a commitment to trauma-informed and inclusive teaching, and a genuine desire to make yoga more accessible.

You don't need to have all the answers or a perfect business plan. If you have the teaching skills and the drive, I can help you figure out the rest.
