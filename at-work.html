Workplace yoga

Reduce stress and energize your team with onsite yoga classes.

Regular yoga sessions help employees manage stress, prevent burnout, improve focus, and build genuine connection. I bring everything needed to run classes in your office, showroom, or workspace.

Why workplace yoga?

The benefits of workplace yoga go far beyond physical fitness. When you invest in regular yoga classes for your team, you're investing in stress reduction, better focus, injury prevention, and a culture that values wellbeing.

Employees who practice yoga together report feeling more connected to their colleagues, more resilient during busy seasons, and more energized throughout the workday. It's a tangible way to show your team you care about their health — not just their productivity.

How it works

I offer recurring weekly or biweekly classes that fit into your team's schedule — lunchtime sessions, morning classes before the workday begins, or evening wind-downs. Each class is 45–60 minutes.

Classes are designed for all levels, from complete beginners to experienced practitioners. No one needs special clothes or prior experience. I provide mats and any other props needed.

I work with companies, retail spaces, showrooms, and coworking spaces across the Portland metro area.

What your team gets

  • Reduced stress and better nervous system regulation
  • Improved posture and reduced desk-related pain
  • Increased energy and mental clarity
  • Stronger team connection and morale
  • Accessible movement for all bodies and abilities

I adapt each class to the space available and the needs of your team. Whether you have a dedicated studio space or a cleared conference room, we'll make it work.

Space requirements

You don't need a fancy studio or dedicated yoga space. A conference room, open office area, showroom floor, or even a large hallway works perfectly. Each person needs about 6 feet by 3 feet of clear floor space.

I bring all the equipment (mats, blocks, straps, blankets) and handle setup and breakdown. All you need to do is clear the space and let your team know when class starts.

Ongoing classes

Weekly or biweekly yoga sessions on a recurring schedule. Best for teams who want to build a sustainable wellness practice.

Workshops

Single-session workshops on specific topics like stress management, desk yoga, or breathwork. Great for team-building days or wellness events.

Custom programs

Multi-week series designed around your team's goals — injury prevention, mental health support, pre-event stress relief, or post-busy-season recovery.
