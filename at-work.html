Corporate & workplace wellness

At work

Elevate performance. Reduce burnout. Strengthen teams.

Yoga To You PDX delivers corporate yoga and workplace wellness programs directly to your office, campus, or job site. We partner with Portland companies to improve employee well-being, reduce stress, and build stronger teams.

Designed for real work

Our corporate yoga programs are designed for real work environments — from offices and warehouses to outdoor campuses. Weekly classes, mobility and injury-prevention, stress reduction, team-building.

Modern teams face stress, long hours, and physical strain. These programs address both mental and physical performance: stress reduction and mental clarity, injury prevention and mobility, focus, and stronger team connection.

What a program can include

  • Weekly or recurring yoga classes
  • Mobility and injury-prevention sessions
  • Stress reduction and mindfulness
  • Team-building wellness experiences

We come to you anywhere in Portland. All mats and props included. Experienced, professional instructors. Customized programming for your workforce.

Why teams book this

Boost energy and release mid-day work fatigue. Stretching and breathing combined with mindful movement restore energy in a healthy way. We handle setup. Your team shows up.

From Nike and the Portland Trail Blazers to offices across the city — same idea: yoga that fits the workday, not the other way around.
