Yoga To You PDX

About

Founded by Lauren Fields.

Yoga To You was created to remove the barriers of traditional studios and reimagine how yoga fits into everyday life. Instead of asking clients to come to us, we bring everything needed for a seamless experience, anywhere. From living rooms and office spaces to rooftops, retreats, and paddleboards.

Lauren

For over 10 years, Yoga To You PDX has been bringing yoga directly to people where they live, work, and gather. What started as a simple idea has grown into Portland's mobile yoga experience, built on accessibility, personalization, and community.

Yoga To You PDX is led by founder and advanced instructor Lauren Fields, whose vision has shaped the company. With over a decade of experience teaching and building community through movement, Lauren brings a personal and intentional approach to every part of the work. Her philosophy is rooted in accessibility — creating spaces where people of all levels feel welcomed, supported, and empowered in their practice.

Yoga isn't about perfection, it's about presence.

The team

Yoga To You is more than one teacher. Sessions are led by experienced, highly trained instructors. We believe yoga is not one-size-fits-all. Every session is thoughtfully designed around the individual or group — a beginner exploring yoga for the first time, a corporate team focused on wellness, or a group celebrating a meaningful moment together.

Our approach blends movement, breathwork, and mindfulness into an experience that is both grounding and energizing. The goal is simple: a space where people feel comfortable, supported, and empowered to move at their own pace.

How we teach

We teach the Fields Method. It is how we work — not a sister brand, and not something you book separately. It sits under every session.

Movement that supports everyday performance. Strength, mobility, and recovery. Accessible. Mind-body work without the complexity.

Every session includes experienced instructors, mats and props, and a thoughtfully designed experience rooted in that approach: safe, effective, and personalized movement.

The school

Teacher training lives at Movement & Yoga Academy, Lauren's school. It is a different door from booking a class — for people who want to teach, and for gyms or studios that want to host a training.
