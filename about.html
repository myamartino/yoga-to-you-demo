About Yoga To You

Accessible, personal, and trauma-informed yoga — wherever you are.

We believe yoga should meet you where you are: in your living room, at your office, or in your community. No intimidating studios, no pressure to perform. Just thoughtful, individualized practice.

Meet Lauren

I'm Lauren, the founder of Yoga To You. I've been teaching yoga for over 12 years, and in that time I've learned that the most transformative yoga happens when people feel safe, seen, and supported.

After teaching in traditional studios for years, I realized that many people who could benefit most from yoga never walked through those doors. So I started bringing yoga to them — in their homes, workplaces, and communities.

I hold a 500-hour yoga teacher certification (RYT 500), specialized training in trauma-informed practices, and certifications in restorative yoga, yin yoga, and mindfulness. I've worked with everyone from complete beginners to seasoned practitioners, from corporate teams to community organizations.

Our approach

Every body is different. Every person has unique needs, goals, and experiences. That's why every session with Yoga To You is customized from the ground up.

I use a trauma-informed approach that prioritizes consent, choice, and accessibility. I offer modifications for all poses and never push students beyond their comfort zone. The goal is to help you build a sustainable practice that feels good in your body.

Whether you're managing stress, recovering from injury, looking for gentle movement, or deepening an existing practice, we'll design sessions that work for you.

The method

Yoga To You sessions blend traditional yoga philosophy with modern understanding of anatomy, nervous system regulation, and trauma-informed care. Each class includes:

  • Breathwork and centering practices
  • Mindful movement tailored to your needs
  • Options for every experience level and ability
  • Rest and integration time
  • Optional meditation or visualization

I provide all props (mats, blocks, bolsters, blankets, straps) and adapt to your space — whether that's a living room, conference room, outdoor park, or event venue.

Beyond private sessions

In addition to Yoga To You, I'm also the founder of Movement & Yoga Academy, a teacher training program for aspiring yoga instructors who want to teach with the same trauma-informed, accessible approach.

If you're interested in deepening your own practice through teacher training, or if you're looking to bring more trauma-informed practices into your existing teaching, visit the Academy page to learn more.
