Teacher training

This is the school.

Teacher training does not live on the Yoga To You booking site. It lives at Movement & Yoga Academy — Lauren Fields' school. A different door, for people who want to teach and for gyms or studios that want to host a training.

This is a demo page so you can see how the school sits next to Yoga To You. The academy's own website is not part of this demo, and we are not sending you to a dead link.

Two paths. One school.

Movement & Yoga Academy is the education side of Lauren's work. Yoga teacher training where you are — in Portland, or on-site with a partner who already has the room.

Take a training

For people launching a teaching practice. Portland-based teacher training, rooted in how we already teach — the Fields Method, movement that supports everyday performance, strength, mobility, and recovery.

The live Yoga To You site already names a 200-hour yoga teacher training. Programs are Yoga Alliance–oriented. Details belong on the school's own site, when that site is back.

Bring a training to your gym

You provide the space. The school handles trainers, curriculum, materials, and assessments. White-label teacher training for studios, gyms, and wellness brands that want education under their own roof.

This is a conversation with the school, not a class booking on Yoga To You.

How this relates to Yoga To You

Yoga To You is the consumer service — yoga that comes to your home, workplace, or event. Movement & Yoga Academy is the school. The Fields Method is how we teach, not a third brand you book on a Saturday.
