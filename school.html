Movement & Yoga Academy

Yoga teacher training for the modern world.

The Movement & Yoga Academy is a trauma-informed, inclusive teacher training program for aspiring yoga instructors who want to teach yoga that's accessible, ethical, and rooted in real understanding.

This is a demo page. The Academy is a real program but not currently enrolling. Contact Lauren for details.

Why this training is different

Most yoga teacher trainings focus on poses, sequences, and philosophy — but they don't prepare you to work with real people in real situations. This training does.

We emphasize trauma-informed teaching, inclusive language, consent-based adjustments, and how to adapt practices for different bodies, abilities, and nervous systems. You'll leave with the skills to teach yoga that's safe, effective, and truly accessible.

The Academy is led by Lauren, founder of Yoga To You, with guest instructors bringing expertise in anatomy, adaptive yoga, business skills, and more.

What you'll learn

  • Foundations of anatomy, alignment, and breath
  • Trauma-informed teaching and nervous system basics
  • How to sequence and adapt classes for all levels
  • Inclusive language and consent-based adjustments
  • Yoga philosophy that's relevant to modern life
  • Business basics: marketing, pricing, and sustainability

This is a 200-hour Yoga Alliance registered program. Graduates are eligible to register as RYT 200 certified teachers.

For new teachers

If you've never taught yoga before but feel called to share this practice, this training will give you a strong foundation in both the art and science of teaching.

We welcome students with all levels of personal practice — you don't need to be able to do advanced poses to become an excellent teacher.

For experienced teachers

If you're already teaching but want to deepen your trauma-informed skills, learn more inclusive practices, or refresh your approach, this training offers continuing education credits and a supportive cohort.

Many experienced teachers join to unlearn outdated practices and build more sustainable, ethical teaching careers.

Program details

The training runs over 10 weekends (Saturday and Sunday, 9am–5pm) plus self-study assignments. We meet in a studio space in Southeast Portland with ample parking and public transit access.

Class size is intentionally small — 12–16 students — to allow for individualized feedback and mentorship. Tuition includes all materials, guest instructor sessions, and ongoing support after graduation.

Payment plans are available. Scholarships for BIPOC students, LGBTQ+ students, and students with financial need are offered each cohort.
