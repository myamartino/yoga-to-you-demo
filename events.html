Yoga for events

Bring yoga to your retreat, celebration, or community gathering.

Whether you're planning a corporate retreat, wedding weekend, community event, or private celebration, yoga can be a meaningful part of your gathering. I handle all the logistics and adapt to your venue and vision.

Event yoga that fits your occasion

Every event is different, and event yoga should reflect that. A morning yoga session at a corporate retreat has a different energy than a sunset class at a wedding weekend or a community gathering in a park.

I work with you to design a session that matches the tone of your event, the experience level of your attendees, and the time and space you have available. No cookie-cutter classes — just thoughtful, customized experiences.

Where we've taught

Over the years I've led yoga at wineries, breweries, parks, beaches, private estates, conference centers, rooftop terraces, art galleries, and more. Indoor or outdoor, intimate or large-scale — we'll make it work.

I bring all necessary equipment (mats, props, sound system if needed) and handle setup and breakdown so you can focus on hosting your event.

Corporate retreats

Yoga sessions for team offsites, leadership retreats, or company gatherings. Great for team bonding and stress relief.

Private celebrations

Yoga for wedding weekends, milestone birthdays, bachelorette parties, or family reunions. A memorable shared experience.

Community events

Public or private yoga sessions for festivals, fundraisers, wellness fairs, or neighborhood gatherings.

What we provide

  • All mats and props for every participant
  • Professional sound system for larger groups
  • Liability insurance and permits (if required)
  • Flexible session lengths (30 min to 90 min)
  • Adaptable to all experience levels

I also offer add-ons like guided meditation, breathwork sessions, sound healing with singing bowls, or take-home materials for attendees.

Retreats and multi-day events

For multi-day retreats, I can lead multiple sessions throughout the weekend — a morning energizing practice, an evening restorative session, or a midday meditation workshop.

I've worked with retreat centers, private rental homes, campgrounds, and destination venues throughout Oregon and Southwest Washington. Travel outside the Portland metro area is available with advance planning.

Planning your event

To get started, reach out with details about your event: the date, location, estimated number of attendees, and what kind of experience you're hoping for. I'll send over availability, pricing, and ideas for how we can make yoga a meaningful part of your gathering.

For large events or events requiring travel, I recommend reaching out at least 6–8 weeks in advance to ensure availability.
